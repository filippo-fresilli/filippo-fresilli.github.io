Why ciaobricks

Photos in, mosaics out.

🖼️

Any photo

Start from your camera roll. Portraits, pets, landscapes — if it's a picture, it can become a mosaic.

🎨

Your palette

Choose from curated brick palettes or build your own. ciaobricks maps every pixel to the closest tile.

⚡️

Native & fast

Built natively for iOS, so rebuilds are instant and everything feels right at home on your iPhone.

🧱

Build for real

Export a tile-by-tile plan with counts per color, so you can recreate your mosaic with physical bricks.

How it works

Three taps to a mosaic.

  1. 1

    Pick a photo

    Choose any image from your library or snap a new one.

  2. 2

    Choose a palette

    Set your tile size and pick the colors you want to work with.

  3. 3

    Build & export

    Watch it snap together, then share or export your build plan.
